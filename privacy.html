Privacy Notice

How we handle your personal data when you use movingtolondonuk.com. Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who we are

Moving to London UK (movingtolondonuk.com) is run by Thames Advisory Group Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17382744, registered office 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London WC2H 9JQ). We are the controller of the personal data described in this notice.

To ask us anything about your data, send a message through the contact form and put "Privacy" at the start of your message.

What we collect

  • Guide and checklist downloads: your first name and email address, and whether you ticked the box asking for occasional emails.
  • Contact form: your full name, email address, move timeline and message.
  • Technical data: when you visit, our website host and the services our pages load receive your IP address and basic device and browser details. See "Who we share it with" below.

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies, and our own pages don't set cookies.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To send you the guide or checklist you asked for. Legal basis: legitimate interests (you asked for it).
  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask for it, introduce you to London Residential Advisors. Legal basis: taking steps at your request, and legitimate interests.
  • To email you occasional tips about moving to London. Only if you tick the box. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and working. Legal basis: legitimate interests.

Who we share it with

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and stores form submissions for us.
  • Google, because our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • London Residential Advisors Ltd, a separate company with the same director, but only when you send an enquiry through the contact form asking for an introduction. They then use your details to reply to you, under their own privacy notice at londonresidentialadvisors.com/privacy.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, where needed, and public authorities where the law requires it.

We don't sell your personal data.

Transfers outside the UK

Some of these providers, including Netlify and Google, process data outside the UK, including in the United States. When that happens we rely on the safeguards UK law allows, such as the UK–US data bridge or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Download requests: up to 24 months, or until you ask us to delete your details.
  • If you ticked the box for occasional emails: until you unsubscribe.
  • Contact form enquiries: up to 24 months after we last spoke with you. If you become a client of London Residential Advisors, their notice applies to the information they hold.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to move it to another provider. You can withdraw consent to emails at any time. Contact us through the contact form and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Children

This website is for adults planning a move. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
