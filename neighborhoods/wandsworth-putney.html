Neighborhoods / Wandsworth & Putney

Moving to Wandsworth & Putney

Good schools and a towpath commute, for people who left the excitement behind on purpose.

South West London District line

What you'll actually be looking at

Common-side terraces

Victorian houses and conversions near Wandsworth Common and Putney Heath, popular with families.

Riverside mansion blocks

Older buildings along Putney's stretch of the Thames, some with towpath views.

New-build riverside

Recent Wandsworth waterfront developments, trading period charm for river views and amenities.

Who it suits

Families prioritising schools and the commons Rowing and river-life enthusiasts in Putney specifically Buyers wanting Clapham-adjacent life with more space for the money

Getting around

  • District line at East Putney, South Western Railway from Putney and Wandsworth Town, plus buses 37, 39, 170, 220, and 270.
  • About 15 minutes to Waterloo on the fast train, 20 to Victoria via the District line.

The honest take

Why people move here

The commons and the river towpath give this stretch more usable green and open space per resident than almost anywhere else on this list.

What catches people out

It's a large, sprawling area under one label — "Putney" and "Wandsworth" cover genuinely different feels, so the postcode alone won't tell you what you're getting.

Where it sits on price

Broadly comparable to Clapham, with more range — riverside new-build at a premium, common-side terraces closer to Clapham's own level.
