Neighborhoods / South Kensington

Moving to South Kensington

The Lycée, the museums, and the closest thing London has to a French quarter.

West London District & Piccadilly lines

What you'll actually be looking at

Museum-quarter terraces

White stucco conversions a short walk from the V&A and Natural History Museum.

Red-brick mansion blocks

Victorian buildings along Old Brompton Road and Cromwell Road.

Family houses near The Boltons

Some of the largest freehold houses in London, rarely on the open market.

Who it suits

Lycée and international-school families Academics and researchers near Imperial College Buyers wanting museums and green space on the doorstep

Getting around

  • District and Piccadilly lines at South Kensington, plus buses 14, 49, and 345.
  • About 5 minutes direct to Victoria, 15 to Waterloo via a Green Park change.

The honest take

Why people move here

The museum quarter and the Lycée make it the natural landing spot for French and international families — an established, ready-made community.

What catches people out

Cromwell Road itself is a genuine traffic artery. A few streets' distance makes a real difference to noise here.

Where it sits on price

Just below Chelsea and Knightsbridge, comparable to or slightly above Notting Hill — genuinely prime, not a budget alternative to its neighbours.
