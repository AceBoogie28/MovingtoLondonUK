Neighborhoods / Hackney

Moving to Hackney

Creative, fast-changing, and one of the most talked-about areas in the city.

East London London Overground

What you'll actually be looking at

Victorian terraces

The bulk of the housing stock, especially around London Fields and Clapton, similar in character to Islington's.

Ex-council flats & estates

A genuine part of the market, with some well-located, good-value options.

New-build near the Overground

Recent developments clustered around Hackney Central and Hackney Wick.

Who it suits

Creative professionals and young families priced out of Islington or Dalston Buyers wanting park space with a shorter commute than it used to be

Getting around

  • London Overground and National Rail at Hackney Central and Hackney Downs, plus a dense bus network (38, 55, 106, 242, 253, 254).
  • The longest ride on this list into the centre — 20–25 minutes with a change at Liverpool Street or Highbury & Islington.

The honest take

Why people move here

London Fields and Victoria Park give it real green-space credentials, and the food and market culture rivals anywhere in East London.

What catches people out

Coverage varies street to street — check the actual walk time to the Overground, not just the postcode.

Where it sits on price

Below Islington and Shoreditch for comparable stock — one of the areas that's moved fastest on price over the last decade, still catching up to its neighbours.
