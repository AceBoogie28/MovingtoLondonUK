Neighborhoods / Hampstead

Moving to Hampstead

A heath the size of a small town and a village centre that barely feels like London at all.

North London Northern line

What you'll actually be looking at

Village cottages

Small, older houses on the lanes near Hampstead High Street, genuinely rare.

Detached & semi-detached houses

The dominant stock on the roads around the Heath, mostly Victorian and Edwardian.

Mansion flats

Larger purpose-built blocks nearer the tube and the village centre.

Who it suits

Established families wanting space and the Heath Downsizers who still want a house, not a flat Buyers prioritising schools and green space over a short commute

Getting around

  • Northern line, plus London Overground from Hampstead Heath and buses 24, 46, and 268.
  • A genuinely longer ride in — about 20 minutes direct to Waterloo, 22 to Victoria via an Euston change.

The honest take

Why people move here

The Heath itself — 320 hectares of genuinely wild green space inside London, unmatched by anything else on this list.

What catches people out

It's a longer, slower commute into the centre than the postcode's reputation suggests — worth timing yourself before assuming.

Where it sits on price

One of the most expensive parts of North London — priced closer to prime-central addresses than to its immediate neighbours.
