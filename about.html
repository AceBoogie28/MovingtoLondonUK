About Moving to London

Moving should be exciting, not destabilizing.

Why this exists

Alex Carroll has spent over a decade in real estate across Canada and the UK. Having lived in multiple countries himself, he kept running into the same problem: the information relocators actually need is scattered everywhere and nowhere. Moving to London is one place for it — clear road maps and guidance, so the practical side of the move doesn't get in the way of the exciting part.

What this site is (and isn't)

Practical, experience-based guidance for relocating to London — neighbourhoods, paperwork, cost of living, the whole sequence. It's not legal or immigration advice, and anything time-sensitive should be verified against official sources.

How this connects to London Residential Advisors

When you're ready to actually search for a home, London Residential Advisors is who this project refers you to for dedicated buyer-side representation. Alexander Carroll, eXp is a trading style of London Residential Advisors Ltd.
