About Moving to London

Moving to a new city can be one of the most beautiful experiences of your life, and it deserves to feel that way.

Why this exists

I've spent my career in real estate and I've lived in more than one country, so I know how a move can feel. There is so much joy in it. New streets, new people, new food and a version of yourself you haven't met yet. Then the paperwork arrives, with visas and bank accounts and tax and a hundred small things that matter on paper and almost never in daily life.

I started Moving to London to put all of that in one place, in plain language, so the practical side stops getting in the way of the exciting part.

Everyone arrives with a few anchors, the things that make you feel like yourself wherever you are. Mine are good food, the gym and time in nature. The sooner you find your version of those in a new city the sooner it starts to feel like home, and everything else on this site is here to clear the way for that.

What this site is and what it isn't

This is practical guidance from my own experience, covering neighbourhoods, paperwork, the cost of living and the order to do things in. It isn't legal or immigration advice, and anything time sensitive should always be checked against official sources.
