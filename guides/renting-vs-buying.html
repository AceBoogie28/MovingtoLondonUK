The decision most people make too early

[Placeholder] Many relocators default to renting for the first year "to get settled," but for some situations — visa length, family needs, market timing — buying sooner can make more sense. The right call depends on your specific circumstances.

Something that catches a lot of people off guard

[Placeholder] In many countries, buyers are used to having their own dedicated agent — someone whose only job is to represent their interests in a negotiation. In the UK, most estate agents legally represent the seller, not the buyer, even when they're the one showing you the property. That distinction matters a lot in a market as competitive and procedurally complex as London's.

[Placeholder callout] If you're used to having a buyer's agent back home, it's worth understanding how that relationship works — or doesn't — by default in the UK before you start viewing properties.
