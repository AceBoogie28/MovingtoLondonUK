Start with your reason for moving

[Placeholder] The right visa route depends heavily on why you're relocating — a confirmed job offer, a partner or spouse already in the UK, remote work for a non-UK employer, or an intent to invest or start a business will each point toward a different route.

Common routes relocators consider

  • [Placeholder] Skilled Worker visa — for those with a job offer from a licensed UK sponsor.
  • [Placeholder] Global Talent visa — for those recognized as leaders or emerging leaders in specific fields.
  • [Placeholder] Family/partner visas — for those joining a spouse, partner, or family member already settled in the UK.
  • [Placeholder] Ancestry and youth mobility routes — for eligible nationals under specific bilateral agreements.
